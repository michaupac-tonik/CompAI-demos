How pricing works

What determines your Comp AI pricing

Four things shape your price, so your quote covers what you need and nothing more.

Frameworks in scope

SOC 2 alone is priced differently from SOC 2 with ISO 27001, HIPAA or GDPR. Shared controls make each extra framework cheaper than starting fresh.

Company size

Headcount sets how many people, devices and access reviews your program covers.

Timeline

A rush to audit-ready is scoped differently from a steady rollout over a quarter.

Audit and security needs

Whether you need the audit, penetration testing or a trust center changes what's included.
