6:00–7:30

Priorities

"All the issues are worth discussing at some point. To keep the work moving, though, there are five I'd like to settle first:

  1. Pricing. Do we keep the current flow, with the form and a booked call? Pricing is the second most visited page, and from a customer's point of view an estimator or calculator would be a real plus.
  2. Product pages. Every product in the nav leads to the same subpage. On the new site, should the structure stay shared while the content differs per product?
  3. CTAs. 'Book a demo' and 'Get started' both lead to scheduling a call, but they say different things. 'Book a demo' says you'll talk to a consultant first. 'Get started' says you're stepping into the process, with the decision already made. Which one should be the primary action? And should one of them lead somewhere different, like a product demo?
  4. Integrations. Right now we say '580+ integrations', but visitors can't explore them. Do we want to expand on that?
  5. Audience. Who does the homepage speak to first: the founder or the security lead? That sets the hero and the section on who Comp is for."
