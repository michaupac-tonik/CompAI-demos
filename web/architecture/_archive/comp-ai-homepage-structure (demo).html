Comp AI: Homepage structure (v1)

Concept: the homepage is a sales page for the 20-minute demo, which closes at around 40%. It says what Comp AI does in one line, then proves it instead of claiming it: the work disappears (policies, evidence, audit), the proof stays visible (open source, real evidence, Trust Center), and real people sit behind the software (experts in your Slack). The page answers two buyers in order: a founder who needs SOC 2 for the next deal, and a security lead who needs control and evidence. One primary action everywhere: Book a demo.
