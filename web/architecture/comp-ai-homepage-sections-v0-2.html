Comp AI: Homepage sections (v0.2)

The order of sections on the new homepage, before any copy, with our recommended solution for each. Each block says what the section is about, what's there today, why it matters and what we recommend, and lists blockers only where a decision stops us from moving on. Findings on the current page are in the separate audit.

Our approach

  • One primary action: Book a demo, everywhere.
  • One home per topic: How it works is the process, Platform is the modules, Security is why Comp makes you safer and how you can verify it.
  • Proof close to the claims, with the strongest numbers near the top.
  • Explain the product first, then prove it, on a shorter page.
  • Reusable sections that carry over to the Book a demo page and the pages that inherit the new nav and footer.

01

Nav + top bar

Global

About

Global navigation shared by the homepage and the ~100 existing pages that inherit it on 13.11.

Today

Series A bar · Product ▾ (10 modules) · Frameworks ▾ (9) · Customers · Pricing · Sign in · Book Demo

Why it matters & what we recommend

It's the entry point to every page, and it has to stay flexible while new pages are decided.

  • Keep Product, Frameworks, Customers, Pricing and Sign in, with Book a demo as the only button.
  • Add Resources (Compliance Hub, Guides, Tools & Templates, Compare), so the pages that bring organic traffic are one click away.
  • Product menu lists the same modules, with the same names, as the page or the Product overview.
  • Leave a slot for About / Company.
  • Keep the Series A bar until launch, then reuse it for launch news.

Blockers

  • Official product module list and names.
  • Which new pages are in scope (Product overview, About). This sets the final nav.
