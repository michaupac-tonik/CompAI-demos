What is Comp AI?

An AI-native compliance and security platform that does the work: it writes your policies, collects evidence from the tools you use and keeps monitoring after the audit. Real experts back it up in your Slack.

Is Comp AI open source?

Yes. Our agents, integrations and checks are on GitHub, so you can read exactly what runs in your environment.

How does evidence collection work?

You connect your tools once. Agents pull evidence continuously from 580+ integrations and map it to the controls in your framework.

From a short questionnaire about how your company works. You review, edit and approve every policy before it goes live.
