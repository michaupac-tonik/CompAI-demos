Comp AI: Current homepage audit

What trycomp.ai has on the homepage today, section by section, in the order it appears. Each block says what's there, what works and what doesn't. Brand and visuals are out of scope; this is about content, messages and conversion.

Audited 15.10.2026 · desktop 1440px · screenshots from the live page

00

Key findings

Across the page

  • Two primary actions compete. The nav and the stage cards say Book Demo (/demo), while the page carries 7 'Get Started' email forms.
  • The page repeats itself. Evidence collection shows up 3 times (How it works, Platform, Security), the device agent and trust center twice, and the line 'Automate evidence collection, policy generation, and continuous monitoring' is used twice word for word.
  • Product doesn't match the menu. The Product mega-menu lists 10 modules, all linking to /#product, which shows 5. Questionnaires, risk assessments, AI policies and the trust center aren't in that section.
  • Frameworks has no home. The dropdown lists 9+ frameworks, but every link goes to /demo (or /demo/soc2, /iso, /hipaa, /gdpr). There's no framework section on the page, only the step 1 card.
  • Strong proof is hidden. 'SOC 2 Type I in ~10 days' sits inside a closed FAQ answer, '<3 min Slack response' in step 4, the $34M Series A only in the top bar. Nothing turns these into numbers near the hero.
  • Claims don't line up. The meta description promises 'audit and pentest included'; the page never says it, and /pricing says it depends on scope.
  • Pricing is missing. The page never mentions price or how it's set, although Pricing is the second most visited page.
  • It's long. About 12,000px on desktop (13 screens) and 16,500px on mobile (20 screens). How it works alone is 2,700px.
  • Keep: the security section's 'verify, don't trust' argument (open source, GitHub links), the Slack support visual, the FAQ structured data and the logo strip.

01

Top bar + Nav

Global · Product ▾ · Frameworks ▾ · Customers · Pricing · Sign in · Book Demo

On the page

Series A announcement bar (TechCrunch link), two mega-menus, Customers → /case-studies, Pricing → /pricing, Sign in → app, Book Demo → /demo.

Works

Short and clear. One filled button (Book Demo). Mega-menus explain each item with a one-line description.

Issues

  • All 10 Product links go to the same anchor (/#product).
  • All Frameworks links go to a demo page; nothing explains a framework.
  • No Resources entry: Hub, guides and comparisons are footer-only.
  • No About / Company entry.

02

Hero

'Compliance that helps you close deals.'

On the page

G2 4.9/5, headline, subline (SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, GDPR · 580+ integrations · audit-ready), email field + Get Started, four framework badges.

Works

Says who it's for and the outcome in one line. G2 rating is the first thing you see.

Issues

  • CTA is an email form, not the demo the rest of the site pushes.
  • No product visual above the fold.
  • 'Audit-ready' without a time or number (the ~10 days is in the FAQ).
  • Badges are small and read as decoration.

03

Logo strip

'Trusted by 1,000+ companies from startups to enterprise'

On the page

Scrolling marquee of greyscale logos (Persona, Dash Social, Assignar, Dub, Corgi, Transloadit, Better Auth, Opencode, Primer, Modash…).

Works

Right place, right format. 1,000+ is a good number.

Issues

  • Mostly dev-tool startups; no logo signals mid-market or enterprise.
  • No numbers next to it.

04

How it works

5 steps · ~2,700px

On the page

1 Pick your frameworks · 2 Tailored to your business · 3 Continuous evidence collection · 4 1:1 Slack support · 5 Share a live trust center. Each step has a UI card.

Works

Clear sequence. Good, specific visuals (framework progress, integrations graph, Slack thread, trust center).

Issues

  • Very tall: one step per screen.
  • Mixes process with features: Slack and trust center aren't steps.
  • No 'audit' step; the story stops before the outcome.

05

Every stage of growth

Accordion · Startup / Mid-Market / Enterprise

On the page

Three rows, Startup open by default with a pitch, three ticks and Book Demo.

Works

Carries the 'grows with you' message the mid-market push needs.

Issues

  • Mid-Market and Enterprise are hidden behind clicks, so the upmarket story is the least visible.
  • Three identical Book Demo buttons, no different offer per stage.

06

Testimonials

'Trusted by teams who ship fast' · 6 quotes

On the page

Grid of six quotes (Persona AI, Docspring, Luthor AI, Capgo, ShiftControl, SessionLab), then a Get Started form.

Works

Real names and roles. The Persona quote (switched from Vanta, <2 weeks) is the strongest proof on the page.

Issues

  • All equal weight; the best one doesn't lead.
  • No outcomes in numbers, no links to /case-studies.
  • Small companies only.

07

Platform

'The AI-first compliance platform' · #product · 5 cards

On the page

Bento of five modules: evidence collection, vendor & risk monitoring, device agents, pen testing, cloud monitoring.

Works

Each card has a real-looking UI snippet. It's what the nav's Product links land on.

Issues

  • 5 modules here vs 10 in the menu; names differ ('Device agents' vs 'Device management').
  • Subtitle copies How it works word for word.
  • Sits after testimonials, so the product is explained after it's proven.

08

Integrations

'Connect with your existing stack' · 580+

On the page

Grid of logo tiles around the Comp AI mark.

Works

Quick to read. 580+ is a strong number.

Issues

  • Logos repeat (Slack twice) and the stacks buyers look for (AWS, GCP, GitHub, Okta) aren't in view.
  • No link to a full list.

09

Security

'Compliance that actually improves your security' · 6 points

On the page

Numbered list: fresh evidence, tailored policies, device agent, tests you write yourself, live trust portal, open source. GitHub and trust-center links.

Works

The most distinctive copy on the site: 'you verify it'. Speaks to security leads and developers.

Issues

  • Text-only, ~1,700px of paragraphs.
  • Half of it repeats earlier sections (evidence, device agent, trust center).
  • Positioned against 'most platforms' without naming them.

10

FAQ

7 questions · 3 groups

On the page

Platform / How it works / Auditing tabs with accordions. FAQPage structured data.

Works

Answers are short and direct ('Yes, 100%' open source; auditor writes the report).

Issues

  • The best number (~10 days to SOC 2 Type I) is buried here.
  • No pricing, switching or 'vs Vanta/Drata' question.
  • The 'How It Works' tab link is broken (#how it works, with spaces).

11

Final CTA

'Don't let compliance slow down your pipeline'

On the page

Dark card, one line of copy, email + Get Started.

Works

Closes on the 'close deals' thread from the hero.

Issues

  • Email form again, not the demo.
  • Nothing new to say; no reassurance (20 min, no commitment).

12

Footer

Product · Resources · Guides · Compare · Legal

On the page

Address (Miami HQ, New York), socials, 5 link columns, compliance badges, 'All Systems Normal' status.

Works

Holds every SEO page (guides, Vanta/Drata comparisons). Status pill is a nice trust touch.

Issues

  • Product column has 3 links (How it works, Platform, Book a demo) vs 10 modules in the nav.
  • No Frameworks column, no About.
