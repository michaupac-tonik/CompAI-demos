How the new homepage is put together

This is the structure of the new homepage, section by section, from top to bottom. On the left of each section you'll find what it's for, what's on it, what changes compared with today, and where we need your input. On the right is a simple sketch of the section.

Grey and plain on purpose. Colours, fonts and visuals come with the new brand. Here we agree on what goes where.
Text is a draft. Headlines and copy show the intended message and will be finalised with the messaging framework.
Grey boxes are images, product screens or logos to come.

New Resources menu, so guides, comparisons and templates are one click away instead of footer-only.
